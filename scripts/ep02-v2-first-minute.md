## // 00 — first 30 seconds (one scene, `engine/ep02v2/scene00.html`)

VO:
Do you remember your first fight? School. A bar. The street.
Remember what it felt like? Panic. Your heart slamming. Your hands shaking. Your mouth going dry. Everything went quiet… and it felt like slow motion.
Here's the crazy part. You didn't choose any of that. Your body made every one of those decisions before you could even think. And one of them… is a lie. Stay — I'll show you which one.
This is what happens inside you in the first ten seconds of a fight. Second by second.

## Fact notes
- "One of them is a lie" = slow motion. Time doesn't actually slow down in a fight; it's a memory effect. Eagleman et al. 2007 (PLoS ONE): 31 m free fall, people rated their own fall ~36% longer than others' falls, but could not read numbers on a wrist display that were too fast to read at normal speed. Paid off later in the episode.
- Heart rate can roughly double in seconds under acute threat (adrenaline). Hedge exact numbers.
