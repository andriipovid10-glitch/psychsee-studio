what the stress response does to your muscles

Stress hormones send more blood to the big muscles of the legs, chest and arms, and muscle tension rises — ready to fight or run.

Meanwhile fine motor control tends to get worse: small, precise finger movements become shaky and clumsy.

Source: Harvard Health Publishing — “Understanding the stress response”
