endorphin

/ɛnˈdɔːr·fɪn/  ·  noun  ·  short for “endogenous morphine”

The body’s own painkillers. Opioid chemicals the brain releases under stress and pain —

the same family as morphine. Scientists call the effect stress-induced analgesia.

Sources: Cleveland Clinic — “Endorphins”; Bandura et al. (1988)
