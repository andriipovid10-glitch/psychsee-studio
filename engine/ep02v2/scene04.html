tunnel vision

Under extreme threat, attention narrows onto the threat — the weapon, the hands, the face.

What sits at the edges is simply not seen.

Source: A. Artwohl (2002), “Perceptual and Memory Distortion During Officer-Involved Shootings”, FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin
